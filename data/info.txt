==================================================
DATASET: weather/weather_data.csv
==================================================

DESCRIPTION
Hourly mean wind speed and air temperature for the 
entire year of 2025, measured at Station SN12680 
(Lillehammer - Sætherengen).

FILE FORMAT
- Type:      CSV
- Separator: Semicolon (;)
- Decimal:   Dot (.)

COLUMNS
- Name                        [str]
- Station                     [str]
- Time(norwegian mean time)   [str]   (Format: DD.MM.2025 hh:mm)
- Air temperature             [float]
- Mean wind speed             [float]
==================================================
